## 4. Discipline & Rebalancing Rules
- **STP Execution**: Initiate all three equity STPs same day; liquid fund = parking vehicle only.
- **SIP Auto-Debit**: Mandate date 1st-5th monthly; ensure folio mapping matches existing direct folios.
- **Quarterly Review**: Check equity/debt drift >5% from 85/15 → rebalance via fresh flows only (no redemption).
- **Annual Step-Up**: Increase SIP 10% YoY; allocate increment pro-rata to maintain sleeve weights.
- **Tax Harvest**: FY-end review — book losses in taxable lots to offset gains; never churn for alpha alone.
- **Exit Load Guard**: No redemption <365 days in equity funds; debt fund exit load nil after 15 days.
